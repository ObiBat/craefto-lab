1

Introduction & Rapport

Set the tone and build initial connection

5 minutes

Objectives

  • 1
    Warm welcome and thanks for their time
    First impressions matter — be genuine and energetic
  • 2
    Brief personal connection
    "Tell me a bit about yourself and your role at [Company]"
  • 3
    Set the agenda
    Give them a roadmap of what to expect
Opening Script
"Thanks so much for taking the time today, [Name]. I'm excited to learn more about what you're building. Here's what I'm thinking for our time together: • First, I'd love to hear about your business and what's driving this project • Then I'll ask some questions to make sure I understand your needs • I'll share a bit about how we might approach this • And we'll figure out next steps together Sound good? Great — so tell me a bit about yourself and [Company]..."
2

Their Story

Understand their business, context, and motivation

20 minutes

Business Understanding Questions

  • 1
    "Walk me through your business — what do you do and what makes you different?"
    Let them explain in their own words. Take notes on exact phrases they use.
    Essential
  • 2
    "Who's your ideal customer? Describe them to me."
    Understanding their audience helps you understand their needs.
    Essential
  • 3
    "Who do you see as your main competitors? What do they do well? Where do they fall short?"
    Reveals market positioning and differentiation opportunities.
    Optional
  • 4
    "What's working well in your business right now?"
    Positive framing builds rapport and reveals strengths to leverage.
    Optional
  • 5
    "What's your biggest challenge right now?"
    Core pain point — listen carefully here.
    Essential
Listen Actively
Use their exact words back to them. "So what I'm hearing is..."
Take Notes
Write down specific phrases and terms they use — use these in your proposal.
3

Project Deep Dive

Uncover specific needs, constraints, and decision factors

20 minutes

Project-Specific Questions

  • 1
    "What specifically are you hoping to achieve with this project?"
    Get concrete outcomes, not vague goals.
    Essential
  • 2
    "If this project is wildly successful, what does that look like in 6 months? In a year?"
    Paints a picture of success you can reference in your proposal.
    Essential
  • 3
    "What happens if you don't do this project? What's at stake?"
    Reveals urgency and helps you understand true priority level.
    Deep Dive
  • 4
    "Have you tried to solve this before? What happened?"
    Learn from past attempts. Avoid repeating mistakes.
    Optional
  • 5
    "Have you worked with agencies or studios before? What worked and what didn't?"
    Critical for understanding expectations and potential red flags.
    Essential

Budget & Decision Questions

  • 1
    "What budget range have you allocated for this project?"
    Ask directly. If they deflect, use the alternatives below.
    Essential
  • 2
    "Have you done similar projects before? What did you invest?"
    Alternative if they won't share budget directly.
    Optional
  • 3
    "Who else is involved in making this decision?"
    Identify all stakeholders. Multiple decision-makers = longer timeline.
    Essential
  • 4
    "What's your timeline for making a decision on this?"
    Helps you prioritize and plan follow-up.
    Essential
  • 5
    "Are you evaluating other studios? What criteria are most important to you?"
    Understand competition and decision factors.
    Deep Dive

If They Won't Share Budget

Say: "I understand budget can be sensitive. Let me share some context — our projects typically range from A$10k for focused brand sprints to A$50k+ for full product builds. Does that range align with what you're thinking, or should we be looking at a different scale?"

Timeline & Logistics Questions

  • 1
    "What's driving your timeline? Any hard deadlines we should know about?"
    External deadlines (launches, events) are harder to move.
    Essential
  • 2
    "Who on your team will we be working with day-to-day?"
    Understand the internal team structure.
    Optional
  • 3
    "What's your preferred way to communicate? Email, Slack, calls?"
    Align on communication early to set expectations.
    Optional
4

Your Value

Show relevant experience and initial thinking

10 minutes

What to Cover

  • 1
    Share 1-2 relevant case studies
    Choose examples most similar to their situation. Less is more.
  • 2
    Initial thoughts on approach
    "Based on what you've shared, here's how I'd think about this..."
  • 3
    What makes Craefto different
    Systems thinking, compound value, craft + strategy.
Positioning Script
"Based on what you've shared, this sounds like a [type of project] challenge. We've helped companies like [relevant example] with similar situations. What I'm thinking is [high-level approach]. The outcome was [specific result]. What sets us apart is our focus on building systems that compound — we don't just solve the immediate problem, we create foundations that continue to deliver value as you grow. Does that approach resonate with what you're looking for?"
Be Specific
Reference their exact situation, not generic capabilities.
Don't Oversell
Confidence, not desperation. You're evaluating fit too.
5

Next Steps

Summarize, align on next actions, and close strong

5 minutes

Closing Checklist

  • Summarize what you heard — "Let me make sure I understood correctly..."
  • Confirm budget range and timeline alignment
  • Propose clear next step — "I'll send you a proposal by [day]"
  • Ask for any final questions
  • Thank them genuinely for their time
Closing Script
"This has been really helpful, [Name]. Let me summarize what I heard: [Summarize their situation and goals] Based on this, I think we could really help you [specific outcome]. Here's what I suggest for next steps: I'll put together a proposal that outlines our recommended approach, timeline, and investment. I can have that to you by [day — within 48 hours]. Does that work? Any other questions before we wrap up? Great — thanks again for your time today. I'm excited about the possibility of working together."

Pre-Call Checklist

Prepare before every discovery call

  • Review their website and any materials they shared
  • Review their intake form responses (if they completed one)
  • Check their LinkedIn profile
  • Identify 2-3 relevant case studies to reference
  • Prepare note-taking setup (Notion, paper, etc.)
  • Test audio/video if it's a video call
  • Block 15 minutes after the call for immediate follow-up notes
