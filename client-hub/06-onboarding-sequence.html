Kickoff Call Agenda

60 minutes

Standard Kickoff Meeting Structure

0-10 min

Welcome & Introductions

Team introductions, roles, and how everyone will be involved

10-20 min

Project Scope Review

Walk through the SOW, confirm deliverables, and clarify any questions

20-30 min

Success Metrics & Goals

Define what success looks like, key metrics, and how we'll measure progress

30-40 min

Timeline & Milestones

Review project timeline, key dates, and any constraints or dependencies

40-50 min

Communication & Workflow

Preferred channels, meeting cadence, feedback process, approval workflow

50-60 min

Questions & Next Steps

Open floor for questions, confirm immediate action items for both sides

Client Onboarding Checklist

Send with welcome email

What We Need From You

  • Complete onboarding questionnaire
    ~10 minutes — helps us prepare for kickoff
  • Provide logo files (AI, SVG, PNG)
    If you don't have vector files, let us know
  • Share brand guidelines (if they exist)
    Colors, fonts, voice/tone documentation
  • Grant platform access
    Domain registrar, hosting, analytics, CMS, etc.
  • Share existing content and copy
    Website copy, product descriptions, team bios, etc.
  • Confirm primary point of contact
    Who has decision-making authority?
  • Schedule kickoff call
    60 minutes within the first week
