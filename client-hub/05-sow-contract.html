Terms & Conditions

Standard Terms for Creative Services

1. Payment Terms

1.1 Deposit. A 50% deposit is required before work begins. Work will not commence until deposit is received.

1.2 Payment Schedule. Payments are due Net 14 from invoice date unless otherwise specified in the SOW.

1.3 Late Payments. Overdue invoices will incur a late fee of 2% per month. Provider reserves the right to suspend work on accounts more than 30 days overdue.

1.4 Expenses. Any pre-approved expenses (stock photography, fonts, third-party services) will be billed at cost plus 15% administrative fee.

2. Intellectual Property

2.1 Work Product. Upon receipt of full payment, Client shall own all rights, title, and interest in the final deliverables created specifically for Client under the SOW.

2.2 Pre-existing Materials. Provider retains all rights to pre-existing materials, tools, and methodologies. Client is granted a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use such materials as incorporated in the deliverables.

2.3 Work in Progress. All work in progress, concepts, and rejected designs remain the property of Provider until full payment is received.

Important: Intellectual property rights transfer ONLY upon full payment. If payment is not completed, Provider retains all rights to the work.

2.4 Portfolio Rights. Provider reserves the right to display work in portfolios, case studies, and marketing materials unless otherwise agreed in writing.

3. Scope Changes

3.1 Change Orders. Any changes to the agreed scope must be documented in a written Change Order signed by both parties before additional work begins.

3.2 Impact. Changes may affect project timeline and budget. Provider will provide estimates before proceeding with any change.

3.3 Out-of-Scope Requests. Requests outside the SOW scope will be quoted separately as new work items or Change Orders.

4. Timeline & Delays

4.1 Client Delays. Delays caused by Client (late content, feedback, approvals, or access) will extend the project timeline by an equivalent period.

4.2 Provider Delays. Provider will communicate any anticipated delays promptly and work to minimize impact on the overall schedule.

4.3 Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by circumstances beyond their reasonable control.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Confidential Information. Both parties agree to keep confidential any proprietary or sensitive information shared during the project.

5.2 Exceptions. Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that: (a) is publicly available, (b) was known prior to disclosure, or (c) is required to be disclosed by law.

6. Termination

6.1 By Client. Client may terminate this agreement with 14 days written notice. Client will be invoiced for all work completed to date.

6.2 By Provider. Provider may terminate if Client fails to make payment within 30 days of due date, or materially breaches this agreement.

6.3 Kill Fee. If Client terminates for convenience (not due to Provider breach), a kill fee equal to 25% of the remaining contract value applies.

6.4 Deposits. All deposits are non-refundable except in cases of Provider breach.

7. Liability

7.1 Limitation. Provider's total liability under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client under the relevant SOW.

7.2 Exclusions. Provider shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages.

7.3 Third-Party Services. Provider is not responsible for the performance, reliability, or actions of third-party services, platforms, or vendors.

7.4 Client Content. Client is responsible for ensuring they have rights to all content, materials, and assets provided to Provider.

8. General Provisions

8.1 Independent Contractor. Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship.

8.2 Entire Agreement. The SOW and these Terms constitute the entire agreement and supersede all prior discussions or agreements.

8.3 Amendments. Changes to this agreement must be made in writing and signed by both parties.

8.4 Governing Law. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of [State/Territory], Australia.

8.5 Dispute Resolution. Any disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation. If unresolved, disputes shall be submitted to mediation before any legal action.
