Pro Tips for Lead Response
  • Speed matters: Responding within 2 hours increases close rates by up to 35%. Set up mobile notifications for new leads.
  • Personalize the hook: Always reference something specific from their inquiry — it shows you actually read it.
  • One call-to-action: Each email should have one clear next step. Don't overwhelm with options.
  • Follow up persistently: Research shows 4-7 follow-ups yield 27% reply rates vs 9% for 1-3 emails.
  • Know when to stop: After 3 follow-ups with no response, mark as lost and move on. Your time is valuable.
  • Track everything: Log every interaction in your CRM. Future-you will thank present-you.
