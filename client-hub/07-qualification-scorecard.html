Score Interpretation Guide

Excellent Fit (80-100)

  • Prioritize and fast-track this lead
  • Schedule discovery call within 24-48 hours
  • Prepare customized proposal immediately
  • Consider premium pricing tier
  • Assign senior team members

Good Fit (60-79)

  • Proceed with standard qualification process
  • Schedule discovery call within 1 week
  • Address any yellow flags proactively
  • Use standard pricing structure
  • Clarify expectations early

Moderate Fit (40-59)

  • Proceed with caution
  • Identify and address concerns before proposal
  • Consider if scope can be adjusted
  • May require additional discovery
  • Set clear boundaries and expectations

Poor Fit (0-39)

  • Politely decline or refer to better-suited partner
  • Document why it's not a fit for future reference
  • Offer helpful resources or alternatives
  • Keep relationship positive for future
  • Don't invest significant time

Bonus Considerations (Not Scored)

Portfolio Value

  • Would this project enhance our portfolio?
  • Is the brand/company recognizable?
  • Could we showcase this publicly?
  • Does it demonstrate new capabilities?

Long-term Potential

  • Is there potential for ongoing work?
  • Does the client have multiple projects?
  • Are they in a growing industry?
  • Could they become a retainer client?

Learning Opportunity

  • Will this expand our team's skills?
  • Is it an interesting technical challenge?
  • Does it align with strategic goals?
  • Would team members be excited?

Referral Potential

  • Are they well-connected in their industry?
  • Have they worked with agencies before?
  • Do they openly share recommendations?
  • Is their network our target market?
